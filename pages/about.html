About StudyFlow

A clean, distraction-free study planner that helps you organize subjects, plan tasks, track exams, and monitor your progress — all in one place, right in your browser.

What you can do

Dashboard

See today's tasks, upcoming exams, progress and a daily quote at a glance.

Task Management

Add, edit, complete and delete tasks. Search, filter by subject and sort by due date.

Calendar

Visualize tasks and exams across the month and jump into any day's details.

Progress Tracking

Watch completion rings and per-subject bars update as you finish your work.

Notes

Keep study notes linked to subjects and find them instantly with search.

Dark / Light Mode

A comfortable theme for any time of day — your choice is remembered.

Built with

No frameworks, no build step — just the fundamentals of the web.

HTML5 CSS3 Vanilla JavaScript Local Storage Flexbox & Grid Responsive

Your data & privacy

Everything you enter is stored only in your browser's Local Storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server — your study data stays entirely on your device.
