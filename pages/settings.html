Keyboard Shortcuts & Help

Quick keyboard references to navigate StudyFlow efficiently without reaching for the mouse.

⌘ K / Ctrl K Global
Open quick search to jump directly to any task, subject, or note across StudyFlow.
Esc Global
Instantly close any active modal, detail overlay, or global search dialog.
Space Focus Timer
Start or pause the focus countdown timer (when no text input is active).
← → ↑ ↓ Calendar
Navigate days with roving focus across the academic calendar grid.
Enter / Space Calendar & Notes
Open the day detail modal for the focused calendar day, or view note details.
⌘ Enter / Ctrl Enter Notes
Quickly save note changes while typing inside the note editor modal.
Privacy & Data Safety Note: StudyFlow runs 100% locally in your browser. No accounts, telemetry, or remote servers store your information. Your planner is preserved in HTML5 LocalStorage. Periodically export a backup if you clear browser data or switch devices.
